Alder · family design language · v1.1.0

Quiet.

Confidence, without volume. One sans-serif, warm paper, a single deep green and an almost obsessive respect for the line. A complete language for a family of applications — captured light and dark, for a design agent to build from.

ModesLight + night
AccentOne green, used rarely

01

The idea

The interface recedes; the information does not. These six principles decide everything that follows.

01

Confidence through omission

Every element earns its place. Try removing something before adding a border, a background or an icon.

02

Type carries hierarchy

Size, weight, measure and space do the work. Colour distinguishes meaning, not sections.

03

A line, not a shadow

Cards are containers, not floating objects. Elevation is reserved for things that genuinely sit above the page.

04

One accent, spent rarely

The green marks action, focus and live data. It is never wallpaper, and never a second brand colour.

05

Motion settles

120–280 ms, one easing. Movement explains a change; it never performs. Nothing loops for decoration.

06

Accessible by default

AA contrast, visible focus, complete keyboard paths and reduced motion are material, not a review step.

One representative screen, composed with the whole language. The family grammar is the same in every app; the nouns change.

Elevation

e0 · card

e3 · dialog
Cards, tables and inputs are e0: a 1px line, 8px radius and space. Shadows are reserved for transient surfaces.

Measure: content max 1180px · prose 62ch · rail 68px · sidebar 236px · top bar 56px.

Density: comfortable 44px rows / 34px controls; compact 36px / 30px. Never mix within a form.

07

The accent policy

One green for the whole family. It marks action, focus and live data — never the primary button, never a status, never an app.

Action · focus

Where you can act

Links, focus rings, the active section and selected rows. The accent says “this is live”.

View statement Payee
Data

What is moving

The primary series in a chart and a highlighted value. One accent line; the rest is muted.

Primary series
Never

Not a button, not a status

Primary actions stay ink or paper. Positive, negative and warning keep their own semantics.

Identity lives in the monogram, not the accent. If you later want per-app orientation, tint only the monogram tile and the 2px active indicator. Never tint buttons, links, charts, status, selection or focus — those belong to the family accent, where colour has meaning.

09

Handoff

The reference is the proof; the tokens and the brief are the contract. Give a design agent the brief and the token file first.

This pack is deliberately technology-agnostic. It describes roles, behaviour and constraints — not React, Swift, Tailwind or CSS. The reference page is a rendering to compare against, not the source of truth.
